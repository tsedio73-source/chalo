Under the hood

Built for a live fleet

Everything a small ride-hailing operation actually needs, nothing it doesn't.

● Live

Encrypted GPS tracking

Driver location streams in continuously and is AES-GCM encrypted client-side before it ever touches the database — so raw coordinates never sit in plain sight, even in the DB console.

Alerts

Two-layer notifications

In-tab alerts fire instantly while the app is open; a background push server delivers real notifications even when the phone is locked or the tab is closed.

Routing

One-tap directions

The moment a driver accepts, a Directions button opens Google Maps straight to the pickup pin — then automatically switches to the drop once the ride is under way.

Fleet

Full admin oversight

A live map of every online driver, ride history with status and revenue, and the ability to ban riders or drivers who misbehave — all in one PIN-gated panel.
